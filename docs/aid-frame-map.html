CarPlay map and native guidance on the AID

What works in the car, where the 800×480 CarPlay frame sits on the 1440×540 AID panel, and which core.so variant gives which layout. The model and the position-marker placement are calibrated on photos taken in the car on 23–24 Oct 2026.

Status in the car

Works

  • CarPlay map in the AID map window (Apple Maps and Google Maps, big and small dials), variant url-map-hybrid: map only, no maneuver card and no speed-limit sign from the iPhone; signs come from the VW camera. The default map-clean has the same layout.
  • Native VW maneuver arrow with distance to the turn in the right dial (appears once moving; when standing at the start of a route there is no maneuver yet).
  • Native distance and time to destination in the bar above the map and in the left dial.
  • The map stays up during Apple Maps guidance (NavActiveIgnore).
  • Map zoom with the steering-wheel arrows, track title and artist on the AID, day/night mode.

Packages

  • Stage A: picture on the AID (dio_manager with overlay, the variant's core.so). Switching variant swaps one core.so, then a restart, no "Potwierdz" (confirm).
  • System RG (package E): iAP2 driver, iap2.cfg, guidance reader.
  • Java: nai, np, rg, zoom, dn. Each one: "Wlacz: …" (enable), restart, check, then "Potwierdz" in the same boot.
  • Install card from installer/build_card_install.py and a separate rescue card from installer/build_rescue.py (restores the stock HMI).

To test

  • Variants in the car: card-clean (with maneuver card), map-clean-l40 (shifted slightly left) and map-square (mathsls layout, closer to square).
  • Native arrow with Waze.

    What the model rests on

    The frame is laid 1:1

    The 800×480 stream is not scaled: it sits on the panel pixel for pixel at x 320–1120, y 60–540. Switching dials only narrows the map window: frame columns 65–735 (small) and 148–652 (big), centred on column 400 in both. Rows 65–405 in both views.

    Measured from the stock VW navigation on the AID in the car (21 Oct) and VW screenshots from the internet; accuracy about ±15 px.

    What the car taught us

    • The …/instrumentcluster/map URL works in Apple and Google Maps: no card.
    • Google puts the marker low and to the right in the safe area: the bottom of the safe area must be around row 404, not 450–460.
    • Declaring 240×144 mm did not shrink the Google card (no comparison with 160×96 mm was made).
    • showSpeedLimit=yes shows nothing; showETA=no could not be judged because the card was replaced by the map alone.
    • The distance/time bar and the arrow are drawn by the cluster (BAP) from iAP2 RouteGuidance data; they do not cover the CarPlay picture except for the top strip of the window.
